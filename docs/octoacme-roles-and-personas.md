# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define and coordinate the quality strategy so release readiness is based on evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Own functional, integration, regression, and acceptance test coverage
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Report quality risks and release readiness
- Coordinate test plans, smoke checks, and production verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on testability, defects, and validation steps
- Partners with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user-facing quality
- Reports readiness and risks to Project Managers for delivery and escalation decisions

---

## UX / Content Designer

### Role Summary
UX/Content Designers shape user workflows, interaction patterns, and content clarity so solutions are usable and understandable.

### Responsibilities
- Define user journeys, interaction flows, and content structure
- Translate customer needs into design direction and usability feedback
- Provide implementation guidance and validate usability before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on user needs, outcomes, and priorities
- Works with Developers to ensure designs are feasible and implemented as intended
- Supports QA by clarifying expected user experience and acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects provide technical direction and manage risks affecting scalability, maintainability, and delivery quality.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, design trade-offs, and technical standards
- Identify dependencies, integration risks, and simplification opportunities
- Review major technical decisions and help estimate complexity
- Escalate technical risks that affect scope, timing, or quality

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Developers make sound implementation decisions
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility and business/technical trade-offs
- Keeps Project Managers informed about dependencies, risks, and execution impact

---

## Release / DevOps Lead

### Role Summary
Release/DevOps Leads coordinate environment readiness, automation, deployment, observability, and rollback planning.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines and release readiness checks
- Coordinate staging and production deployments
- Validate post-deploy health and operational readiness
- Support rollback decisions and incident follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and QA on release criteria and validation
- Works with Project Managers on timing, communications, and incidents
- Supports Product Managers with release status and customer-impact information

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Partners identify security, privacy, and compliance risks early and help teams implement appropriate controls.

### Responsibilities
- Guide threat modeling, risk assessments, and control validation
- Define review gates for sensitive data and high-risk functionality
- Partner on mitigations and escalate unresolved risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and Technical Leads on secure implementation
- Supports Product Managers in assessing customer and business risk
- Keeps Project Managers informed about remediation, escalation, and governance needs

---

## Business / Subject-Matter Expert

### Role Summary
Business/Subject-Matter Experts provide domain knowledge so decisions reflect real workflows, policies, and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business processes, rules, and operational constraints
- Validate workflows, edge cases, acceptance criteria, and outcomes
- Provide feedback on usability, adoption, and operational impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine requirements and value outcomes
- Helps Developers understand real-world behaviors and constraints
- Supports Project Managers by improving stakeholder alignment and reducing ambiguity

---

## Operations / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Operations/Support Representatives ensure solutions are supportable and operationally ready after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Represent support, runbook, onboarding, and troubleshooting needs
- Contribute to production readiness and incident response planning
- Monitor customer-impacting issues and provide post-release feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Release/DevOps Leads on readiness and rollback planning
- Supports Product Managers with customer impact and issue-prioritization insights
- Helps Project Managers coordinate launch communications and follow-up actions

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, resource support, and decision-making authority for cross-functional work.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priority, business case, and resource support
- Remove organizational barriers and unblock dependencies
- Review major risks, trade-offs, and decision gates
- Support escalation decisions for business-impacting issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides strategic context and support to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Receives concise project health and risk updates from Project Managers
- Resolves cross-functional barriers that delivery teams cannot address themselves

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Tailor persona coverage to the scope, size, and risk profile of each initiative; not every project needs every role.
